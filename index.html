About Melanee

A quieter, more personal way to move.

Melanee founded her practice on a simple belief: that buying or selling a home is one of life’s most meaningful decisions, and it deserves an advisor who treats it that way.

She pairs an eye for architecture and design with a deep understanding of local markets — and, above all, a commitment to listening. Her clients come to her for expertise, and stay for the relationship.

Work with Melanee

“I don’t measure success by the number of homes I sell, but by how my clients feel when they walk through their front door.”

— Melanee
Her Approach

Guided by three principles

i.

Listen first

Every search and sale begins with understanding your priorities, timing and the life you want to build.

ii.

Advise honestly

Clear, candid counsel — even when it isn’t what you expected to hear. Your interests always come first.

iii.

Care for every detail

From presentation to paperwork to closing day, nothing is left to chance and nothing is rushed.

Beyond the Transaction

Rooted in community

Melanee lives and works in the neighborhoods she represents. She knows which streets glow in autumn, which cafés open earliest, and which homes have stories worth telling.

That local fluency — paired with a trusted circle of lenders, inspectors, stagers and designers — makes every move smoother.
